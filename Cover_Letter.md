We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled **”Institutional Portfolio Optimization under Cardinality and Execution Frictions: A Walk-Forward Evaluation of Adaptive Diversity Preservation”** for consideration as a **Research Article** in ***Intelligent Computing*** (a Science Partner Journal published by the **American Association for the Advancement of Science [AAAS]** in affiliation with Zhejiang Lab).

This manuscript presents **Adaptive Opposition-Based Learning Symbiotic Organisms Search (AOBL-SOS)** for high-dimensional, non-convex constrained optimization. The core contributions of this work are:

*   **Algorithmic Architecture:** The formulation of an intelligent continuous-to-discrete search engine combining Symbiotic Organisms Search with an **exact feasibility-preserving projection $\mathcal{M}$** (strictly enforcing $K=30$ cardinality and $W_{\max}=20\%$ caps without heuristic repair) and a **stagnation-triggered centroid opposition operator** tailored to non-convex manifolds.
*   **Benchmark & Real-World Validation:** The realistic evaluation of operational frictions across **seven expanding walk-forward windows (2018–2024)**, incorporating 10 bps annual formation costs and nonlinear **Almgren–Chriss market impact up to $5B AUM**, bridging theoretical evolutionary search and large-scale operational execution.
*   **Risk-Aware Objective Function:** The optimization of a path-dependent fitness function actively penalizing maximum drawdown alongside Sharpe ratio, proving the framework natively navigates severe market regime shifts while preserving candidate feasibility.

Extensive walk-forward evaluations demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves **superior out-of-sample risk-adjusted performance** (chained net Sharpe **0.919**, net CAGR **21.55%**, Calmar ratio **0.616**) and reduced maximum drawdown (**–31.75%** vs –36.05%) across 8 competing baselines. Econometric and statistical analyses (Carhart 4-factor attribution, CSCV PBO, and Jobson–Korkie bootstrap tests) further confirm significant economic outperformance, institutional scalability, and parameter stability. Replication data and results are openly deposited on **Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22274526)**.

We believe that our work aligns closely with the aims of ***Intelligent Computing***, advancing bio-inspired computational intelligence and metaheuristic optimization for high-dimensional, non-convex decision systems. The manuscript will interest researchers and practitioners working in evolutionary algorithms, swarm intelligence, and applied computational optimization.
